TECHNOLOGY

AI grounded in a construction system.

General AI can describe a facade. GFP generates a coordinated one. It converts a building brief into construction operations, resolves the curtain wall and keeps every measurable result connected as the design changes.

01 · ARCHITECTURE

The building logic.

Standard and custom floor heights, slabs, ceiling voids, raised floors, podium-to-tower relationships and setbacks.

02 · CURTAIN WALL

The parts that make it real.

Measurable mullions, transoms and glass panels; vision and spandrel zones; internal and external corners; drainage at junctions.

03 · RATIONALISATION

Every panel is accountable.

Each glass panel is identified, measured, counted and grouped. The engine rationalises panel sizes and calculates glass, aluminium and silicone quantities.

04 · ENGINEERING

Loads remain linked to design.

Wind pressure is resolved across every facade and feeds field loads, glass checks, deflection and profile-property logic.

Depth before breadth

The public engine begins with aluminium stick curtain walls so the construction logic can be complete, measurable and useful. The same architecture can expand into contractor and manufacturer systems as their catalogues, products and rules are encoded.

Working today

Build and revise a stick curtain wall from a prompt; resolve floors, grids, glass, corners, setbacks and drainage; calculate quantities and wind; and produce elevations, plans, sections and visualisations from one live building state.
